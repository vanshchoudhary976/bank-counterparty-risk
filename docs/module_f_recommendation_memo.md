Module F recommendation memo: Costco Wholesale Corp (ILLUSTRATIVE)
Purpose. This memo compares a discounted cash flow value with the share price and states what the credit metrics show about debt capacity. It is a study output, not an investment recommendation, a credit rating or a regulatory rating.
Valuation. The base case gives a value of about $303 per share, against a share price of $1,053.56 (close, 9 Nov 2026). The model is 71% below the price. The assumptions are a WACC of 8.75% and terminal growth of 2.0%. The cost of equity uses a risk-free rate of 5.12% (16 Nov 2026), an equity risk premium of 4.28% (Damodaran, 1 Aug 2026) and a beta of 0.87 (Yahoo Finance, 28 Jul 2026). Across the three scenarios the value is $156 (downside), $303 (base) and $392 (upside). All three are below the share price. For the price to be supported, the forecast would need stronger margins or growth than the 2022 to 2026 history shows, or a lower discount rate. The sensitivity grid is in the workbook.
Credit metrics and debt capacity. Costco has net cash. In FY2026, total debt was $6.2bn and cash plus short-term investments was $21.3bn, so net debt was negative. Net debt to EBITDA was -1.05x. Gross debt to EBITDA was about 0.43x. Interest cover was about 98x in FY2027 in the base case, and 76x in the downside. Free cash flow to debt was 119% in the base case and 70% in the downside. Debt capacity is not a constraint in any scenario.
Limitations. The model does not include dividends, buybacks or debt repayment, so cash builds up over the forecast. This makes the cash-based metrics look stronger than they would be after capital returns. Beta and terminal growth are sourced, but other dated sources give beta between 0.87 and 0.99. The downside and upside assumptions are analyst judgements. The interest yield includes other income.
Recommendation. On these assumptions, the discounted cash flow does not support the current share price. Credit risk is low in all three scenarios, and debt capacity is not a concern. Before the valuation is used, the model needs the sensitivity grid for margin and growth, and a check of the beta range.
